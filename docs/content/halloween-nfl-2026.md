# Halloween extension and NFL collection

On October 23, 2026, the owner requested another Halloween theme starting tomorrow, and one theme for every NFL team with its logo on the cup and recognizable historic or present settings, explicitly Candlestick Park for the 49ers. This authorizes the new Halloween assignment and the 32 built NFL options. November date assignments remain separate.

## Eight Halloween nights

October 24 changes from Edinburgh fireside to a new Guinness at Lantern maze, a painted corn-maze entrance with amber lanterns, pumpkins and a harvest moon. It retains the original Guinness glass, print, physics and scoring target. The published Halloween campaign starts October 24 and explicitly approves this eighth member; October 25–31 stays in the same seven-scene order. The autumn run ends October 23. No assignment through October 23 changes.

## NFL collection

The 32 teams from the [NFL directory](https://www.nfl.com/teams/) each get a distinct newly generated portrait, a clear plastic stadium cup with a self-hosted transparent team logo, an accessible playable review and a matching sample postcard. The directory and exact individual source logo URLs are recorded in `nfl-collection.json` and each print sidecar. Existing current team marks are used even when the painting recalls a historic home, such as the Raiders at Oakland. The gallery labels these as illustrated fan memories; it makes no claim of a scheduled matchup or current venue. No player likenesses are included.

Each painting is one built-in ImageGen generation. The exact prompt and generated path are retained in `halloween-nfl-generation.json` and the shipping WebP sidecar. No original scene is overwritten. Foreground vessels are rendered separately. Scene anchors are measured on the final paintings; failed scenery recovers the pub, and a failed team print retains the team initials with a visible gallery warning.

`THEME_READINESS` records each scene hash and every NFL logo hash. Scheduling checks require both artifacts to match the approval. The NFL themes are available in the review picker and calendar planner but receive no implicit campaign or November date assignment. The six automatic fallback choices and released daily seeds remain fixed.
