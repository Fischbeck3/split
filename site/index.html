How to play

A dashed line briefly shows the target before you sip. Opening these rules shows it again. Hold the glass, button, or space bar to tip and sip. Release a little early to bring it upright; the sip keeps flowing briefly. Match the settled beer line beneath the foam to that target. On Guinness, aim at the top of the G’s crossbar. Your device’s reduced-motion setting keeps the glass upright without changing the sip or scoring.

The line falls faster as the glass narrows. Release before the mark.
