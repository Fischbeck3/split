# NFL game-day assignments, November–December 2026

On October 24, 2026, the owner approved the built NFL collection and requested calendar assignments for relevant matchups, especially major rivalries and primetime games, in November and December. This review assigns 31 standalone game days using 22 of the already built and approved team cups. Each date is an explicit published assignment; these sparse dates do not reserve a two-month campaign or approve any holiday run. Unassigned dates remain unplanned for later seasonal scheduling.

## Selection review

Prioritize national evening windows, classic divisional matchups and holiday standalone games. One cup represents one participating team each day. Rivalry choices take precedence over the night window on November 8 (former Bay Area neighbors), November 29 (Seattle–San Francisco), December 6 (Battle of Ohio) and December 27 (San Francisco–Kansas City, recalling their Super Bowl meetings). This does not attempt to use every team equally or force all 32 into this interval. All 32 remain available for later selections.

The date key is the game’s America/Phoenix date, verified from the kickoff instant. In particular, the Monday evening November 30 game is November 30, even though its UTC kickoff is December 1. No uncertain Saturday/Sunday flex candidates are assigned. The confirmed December 19 Saturday feature is included. The holiday anchors are Packers–Rams on Thanksgiving Eve, Eagles–Cowboys on Thanksgiving, Broncos–Steelers on Black Friday, Texans–Eagles on Christmas Eve, Packers–Bears on Christmas and Ravens–Bengals on New Year’s Eve.

Each selected matchup was checked on October 24 against ESPN’s published weekly fixture data and the linked official participating team schedule. `nfl-calendar-2026.json` records the exact event ID, teams, original kickoff UTC, broadcast, official source and fixture endpoint for each selection. Runtime `site/js/nfl-calendar.js` carries the explicit date/cup assignment and human-readable occasion, selection reason, source and check date. The official source link and check date appear in the day inspector. Kickoff times are evidence, not calendar appointments: Split subscriptions remain all-day content dates.

NFL flex scheduling can move a fixture, including its calendar day. The page and feeds state that limitation and the verification date. These are reviewed Split content assignments, not a promise that a network’s initial game slot cannot change. A future fixture change requires a new calendar review and release; no automatic schedule mutation is introduced. Stadium artwork remains an illustrated fan memory and may depict a historic venue, such as Candlestick, Oakland Coliseum or RFK, rather than the current game venue. The November 22 fixture is explicitly in Mexico City.

## Approval and preservation

The existing collection approval in [Halloween and NFL release](halloween-nfl-2026.md) covers the scene, clear plastic logo cup and matching postcard for every selected theme. This scheduling release reuses those exact approved scene and logo hashes, and runs the same content-readiness checks. Each game is a separately reviewed standalone date (`campaignId: none`); no new themed run is claimed or inferred from a date span. This record is the owner's authorized date-selection review.

The calendar becomes version 6. All October assignments and campaign records remain unchanged. The six automatic fallback choices, all original 82 catalog positions, the 115-theme review catalog and all earlier released challenge parameters remain unchanged. The remaining 30 dates in November and December are unplanned; the built Thanksgiving, Hanukkah and Christmas galleries receive no new assignments from this request.
