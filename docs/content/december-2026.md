# December 2026 holiday collection

On November 6, 2026 the owner requested holiday themes throughout December, adding Christmas lights, decorated trees, and festive cups. They reviewed the six-example design board and said “build em!”. This authorizes the six new festive glass designs and the all-December holiday lineup. The board supplies scene and cup direction; the existing game layout and physics remain the implementation authority.

## Release behavior

Calendar version 7 assigns all 31 December dates to the december-2026 campaign. The prior 15 December NFL assignments are superseded, including Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and New Year's Eve. Their original evidence remains in nfl-calendar-2026.json and nfl-calendar.js as the earlier selection record. No superseded matchup claims are attached to the new holiday entries. All October and November assignments, automatic fallbacks, earlier catalog positions, saved-score rules and released pours remain unchanged.

The six new paintings are Christmas at the local, Market lights, Fireside after the slopes, A very merry game day, Christmas in the desert, and One last holiday toast. Twenty-five existing Christmas paintings receive sibling December entries with those festive cups, making 31 distinct painted settings. The earlier Christmas, winter, Thanksgiving, Hanukkah and NFL review entries retain their original foregrounds and remain available.

Guinness retains its original decoded wordmark, harp and G scoring origin, with holly decoration outside the mark. Other cups use a fir tree, gold star, football laces, red bauble or eight-point midnight star. Their transparent mug, nonic pint, plastic party cup, tumbler and beer chalice use existing vessel profiles. Printed bands are clipped to the moving vessel and away from the seeded mark in play and postcards; the decorative layer cannot change liquid flow, score, or timing.

The gallery at /december.html presents the six designs first, then the remaining dates in order. All gallery links open unsaved #admin previews. Sample postcards remain explicitly illustrative. The calendar, subscriptions, exact-date challenges and theme previews resolve the same theme definitions.
